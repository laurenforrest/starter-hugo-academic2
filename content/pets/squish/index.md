**Squish** (aka Squishy) is the puppiest of all puppies. He is extremely cute. He is adorably clumsy. He loves biting and playing with anything in sight and has a very "...SQUIRREL!" vibe while playing. He is also a hellion. Even though his big sis Lila is at least 6x his size, he is fearless when interacting and playing with her. (And being her perfect Lila self, she is equal parts gentle and playful with him.) In less than 1 week Squishy has learned sit, stay, off, and crate. His smarts remind us of his rainbow bridge brothers, Echo and Jax. Everyone except Mac is enjoying learning about Squish and watching his personality show itself and develop. (Being that Mac is like 80-something years in dog years, he is just too distinguished for puppy antics. 😂)

PS like so many rescue pups, we have no idea what breeds Squish is. We think maybe lab and husky, specifically on account of his ears, tail shape, and general vibe. Maybe time will tell!
